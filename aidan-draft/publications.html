  • Amy Felty, Alberto Momigliano, Brigitte Pientka. The Next 700 Challenge Problems for Reasoning with Higher-Order Abstract Syntax Representations: Part 1 - A Foundational View. This paper highlights some challenge problems for systems reasoning with higher-order abstract syntax and provides in general a good introduction to common issues when reasoning about formal systems.
  • Amy Felty, Alberto Momigliano, Brigitte Pientka. The Next 700 Challenge Problems for Reasoning with Higher-Order Abstract Syntax Representations: Part 2 - A Survey (including the electronic appendix). This paper provides an in depth comparison between different systems supporting HOAS specifications, in particular it contrasts systems built on a type-theoretic foundations such as Twelf and Beluga to systems built on a proof-theoretic foundations such as Hybrid and Abella.
